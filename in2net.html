Akira Mercedes

Creative Director & Brand Designer

📍 London - Bristol

For the past 8 years, I’ve been crafting visual identities and brand systems for companies across fashion, tech, and culture. Currently leading creative at Wolff Olins, previously at Pentagram and IDEO.

I believe great design isn’t just aesthetically compelling — it’s strategically grounded and culturally aware. My approach blends bold typography, thoughtful color systems, and contemporary minimalism to create brands that stand out and resonate.

Beyond client work, I run Grid & Grit, a weekly design newsletter exploring typography, color theory, and design history. It’s read by 8,500+ designers at companies like Apple, Google, and Airbnb.

When I’m not designing, you’ll find me hunting for vintage posters, shooting film photography, or exploring Brooklyn’s independent bookstores with an oat cappuccino in hand.

I’m always open to collaborating on projects that push creative boundaries and challenge conventional thinking.
