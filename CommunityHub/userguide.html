📌 Tab Panels Explained

  • 1
    Pending Requests — Online enquiries from the public form. Review details, then click Confirm (creates a confirmed booking) or Decline.
  • 2
    Upcoming Today — All bookings happening today, sorted by start time. Use this as your check-in list at the start of each shift.

When the dashboard loads, it reads your login token from sessionStorage. The token contains your name, role, and venue ID — all displayed automatically without any extra login step.

If the token is missing, expired, or incomplete, the page immediately redirects you to login.html. This is normal behaviour — simply log in again. No data is lost.

Sessions end automatically when you close the browser tab or window. This is a security feature — avoid leaving the dashboard open on shared computers.

💡 Shift start tip: Open the Dashboard first every shift. Clear all items in Pending Requests before switching to any other page — new enquiries can expire if left unreviewed for more than 48 hours.

1️⃣ Making a Booking — Three Ways

🔗 Copy link

✅ Option A — Calendar Booking

  1. Navigate to Calendar sidebar.
  2. Click date box to open the Quick Booking drawer.
  3. Fill details: Customer, Room, Timings, Guests.
  4. Review indicator: 🟢 Available / 🔴 Clash alert.
  5. Optionally check Multi-day event or add internal staff notes.
  6. Choose payment: Record Deposit, Full Payment, or Save as Pending.

🚪 Option B — Walk-In Booking

  1. Select Walk-In Booking sidebar.
  2. Enter Customer details (Name, Contact).
  3. Input Event parameters: Date, Duration, Guest Count.
  4. Select offline payout choice: Cash, Card, or BACS.
  5. Click Confirm Booking.
⚠️ Walk-ins cannot be booked on past dates.

📨 Option C — Enquiry Form (Online)

A public-facing booking request page customers fill in themselves. Share the link directly, embed it on your website, or use it during a telephone enquiry. URL format: enquiry-form.html?t=<tenant_id>

Fields collected
  • Full Name • Email • Phone
  • Preferred Room (with capacity shown)
  • Event Type • Start & End Time
  • Date (single) or Date From / Date To
  • Number of Guests • Notes (optional)
  • + Additional Rooms (checkbox, optional)
Live features
  • 🗓️ Multi-day toggle — reveals Date From / Date To (max 90 days)
  • 🏷️ Cost estimate — updates as customer types: £X/hr × Xh × N days
  • 🟢 Availability check — real-time; submit unlocks only when slot is free
  • 🚫 Blocked dates, time inversions, and 90-day spans caught before submit
Submission options
Free Enquiry

No payment required. Creates a pending booking request. Customer and staff both receive a notification email. 7-day window to add deposit before expiry.

Pay Deposit by Card

Stripe Checkout — 20% of estimated total (£10–£500). Creates a pending_deposit request. Customer lands on a branded payment confirmation page with their Booking ID.

  • 1
    Click New Booking in the sidebar to open the form in a new tab, or copy the ?t= URL and share it with the customer.
  • 2
    Customer fills in their details, selects room and times. The green availability banner confirms the slot is free before they can submit.
  • 3
    Customer submits via Submit Enquiry (free) or Pay Deposit by Card (Stripe). Both paths send a notification email to staff.
  • 4
    Enquiry appears in Dashboard → Pending Requests. Click the row, review details, then click Confirm to convert it to a confirmed booking (triggers customer confirmation email).
⚠️ Free enquiries expire after 7 days with no deposit. The system sends an automatic warning email at day 4.

4️⃣ Setting Up Recurring Payments & Series

🔗 Copy link

Create a Recurring Series

  1. Open the Calendar and click any available date to open the quick-book drawer.
  2. Fill in customer name, phone, room, and times.
  3. Click Repeat this booking to expand the recurring contract section.
  4. Choose a Frequency — how often sessions occur (see table below).
  5. Select the days of the week the customer attends.
  6. Choose a Contract Duration (3 months, 6 months, or 1 year).
  7. Choose Payment Terms and Cycle Length (see below).
  8. Check the preview — it shows the total session count, end date, and billing summary.
  9. Click Create Series to confirm.

Process / Record Series Payment

  1. Go to Recurring in the sidebar.
  2. Locate the customer's series card.
  3. Check the badge: Paid, Due, or Overdue.
  4. Click Record Payment (shown when a cycle is outstanding).
  5. Enter the amount, select the payment method, and click Confirm Payment.

Frequency vs Cycle Length — what's the difference?

These two fields control different things. Frequency sets how often sessions happen. Cycle Length sets how often the customer is billed.

Field What it controls Options
Frequency How often sessions are scheduled Weekly · Every 2 Weeks (Fortnightly) · Monthly
Cycle Length How often a payment is collected 1 week · 2 weeks · 4 weeks · 8 weeks · 12 weeks

Example: A customer books every Monday (Frequency = Weekly). You set Cycle Length = 4 weeks — so they're billed once every four sessions. You could equally set it to 1 week (pay weekly) or 8 weeks (pay every two months). The sessions don't change, only the payment cadence does.

💡 When Cycle Length is hidden:
  • Pay In Full — only one charge covers the whole contract, so there are no cycles to configure.
  • Monthly frequency — the billing period is locked to one calendar month automatically and cannot be changed.

Frequency options explained

Frequency How sessions are scheduled Example (12-week contract, 1 day/week)
Weekly Same day(s) every week 12 sessions total
Every 2 Weeks (Fortnightly) Same day(s) every other week 6 sessions total
Monthly Same weekday position each month — e.g. if the start date is the 2nd Monday, every subsequent month the 2nd Monday is booked 3 sessions total (one per month)
💡 Monthly booking tip: The system automatically locks to the correct weekday position from the start date. If you open the drawer on a 3rd Thursday, every month will book the 3rd Thursday — you don't need to calculate this yourself. The session preview will show the exact dates before you confirm.

  • 2

  • 3
    Review details: total value, remaining balance, and amount slider field.

📬 Enquiry Received (Customer)

Fires the instant the online enquiry form is submitted successfully. The customer receives this even before staff review it.

  • Summary of the request: space, date, time, event type, guests
  • Clear notice of the 7-day confirmation window
  • Enquiry reference number
  • Contact button → bookings@venuedesk.co.uk

🔔 New Enquiry (Staff Alert)

Fires at the same moment as the customer acknowledgement — no manual check required. Delivered to the address configured in Admin Config → Settings → Staff Notification Email.

  • Customer name, email, and phone
  • Event details: space, type, date, time, guests
  • Enquiry reference ID
  • Review in Dashboard deep link → one click to the pending requests list
⚙️ Where to configure it: Admin Config → Settings tab → Staff Notification Email card. Enter your address and click Save — takes effect on the next enquiry, no restart needed. Defaults to bookings@venuedesk.co.uk if left blank.

Every enquiry has a 7-day window to receive a deposit. The system runs a check daily at 08:00 and sends one warning email to any customer whose enquiry is between 4 and 7 days old with no deposit paid.

Days 1–3Staff review and contact the customer to arrange a deposit

Day 7 (at 08:00)Enquiry is permanently deleted from the system if no deposit has been paid

⚠️ Once an enquiry is purged on day 7, it cannot be recovered. Contact the customer promptly after day 4 if they haven't paid a deposit.

💳 Accounts & Ledger (accounts.html)

The Accounts page consolidates all financial data — cash collected, Stripe online payments, BACS transfers, and outstanding balances.

  • 1
    Select the period using the month picker at the top. The totals update instantly.
  • 2
    The summary row shows: Total Collected · Outstanding · Stripe Processed · Cash/BACS.
  • 3
    Scroll down to see Recurring Series cards — each shows the payment status per session (✓ Paid · ⚠ Due · ○ Upcoming).
  • 4
    Click any booking row to open the full invoice detail and record an additional payment if needed.
  • 5
    Click Export CSV to download the ledger for the selected period — useful for end-of-month reconciliation.

When a customer has a confirmed booking with an unpaid balance, it appears in the Outstanding Payments list on the Dashboard and in the Accounts page with an Payment Due badge.

  • 1
    Open Accounts → locate the booking row flagged Payment Due.
  • 2
    Click the row to open the invoice detail panel on the right.
  • 3
    Click Record Payment. A modal opens showing the outstanding balance.
  • 4
    Select payment method (Cash / BACS / Card Terminal / Stripe Link) and enter the amount received.
  • 5
    Click Confirm Payment. The badge updates to Fully Paid and the Accounts total refreshes automatically.
⚠️ Always verify physical cash or bank confirmation before clicking Confirm — the system records what you enter immediately.

The Final Payment page (final-payment.html) is a simplified one-screen view designed for use at the front desk on the day of an event. It shows only bookings with an outstanding balance for today, making collection fast.

  1. Access via the Final Payment link in the sidebar (visible on the day of a booking).
  2. The page auto-loads all today's bookings that have a remaining balance.
  3. Click Pay Balance on the relevant row, select the payment method, and confirm.
  4. The booking status updates to Fully Paid across the whole system instantly.

⚠️ Troubleshooting Common Problems

🔗 Copy link
Problem / Warning Message Likely Root Cause Actionable Solution
🔴 Unavailable / Red Bar Double-booking detected on room or hour selection. Select an alternative time, change rooms, or verify overlaps in Calendar view.
⚠️ Guest Count Warning Guests exceed capacity limits of selected room. Reduce guests size or change space selection to a larger room.
❌ Cannot book past date Date chosen has already passed. Modify scheduling details to current date or future.
📄 Page blank / list empty Staff authentication token has expired. Refresh page and log back in to reload session.
🔄 "Something went wrong" popup API network connection timed out. Click Refresh in top-right of main control bar.
👤 Customer not found Contact details not added to the database yet. Fill walk-in form (which auto-registers profiles) or click Add Customer in CRM.
💳 Balance still showing Confirmed Payment was not clicked after entering details. Re-open booking profile, press pay and ensure you complete the ledger submission.
🔄 Recurring payment button missing Current period has already been paid or date is too early. Inspect series details and review status badge in recurring widget.
🚪 Logged out unexpectedly Token security timeout expired (1 hour). Re-authenticate credentials. No unsaved database entries will be lost.
🏛️ "Conflict with parent/child room" A partition or parent room is already booked in that exact time slot. Check Calendar for the conflicting booking. Book a non-overlapping partition (e.g. South Half instead of North Half), or choose a different date/time. See Section 6.
🏛️ Booking parent blocks all children Conference Hall booked = no partitions available for same slot. Cancel or reschedule the parent room booking first, then individual partitions will become bookable again.
🕐 "Room does not open until HH:MM" The chosen start time is before this room's configured opening time. Change the start time to be within the room's operating hours. Check Admin Config → Rooms to see each room's open/close times.
🕐 "Room closes at HH:MM" The chosen end time exceeds this room's closing time. Move the end time earlier, or select a different room with a later close time.
Booking duration exceeds 90 days Multi-day booking span is over 90 days. Shorten the booking period or split into two separate bookings.
✉️ Customer got no confirmation email Email landed in spam, or address was entered incorrectly. Ask them to check spam. Verify their email address on the Customers page.
✉️ Customer received duplicate expiry warning emails System test was run with a live email address (rare). Contact your administrator — a workflow was tested against a real inbox.
✉️ Staff alert email not arriving Notification address not configured or wrong address. Go to Admin Config → Settings → Staff Notification Email, verify the address is correct, and click Save. Changes take effect on the next enquiry.
✉️ BACS email has no bank details Venue BACS details not set in Admin Config. Go to Admin Config → Payments tab and enter Sort Code, Account Number, and Account Name.

📖 Key Concepts Glossary

🔗 Copy link
Pending Request

An online booking inquiry submitted by a client awaiting review and formal confirmation by staff.

Confirmed

Booking verified, date reserved, block active on Calendar, and client notified.

Deposit Paid

Partial payment registered to book date. Balance remains outstanding until event check-in.

Fully Paid

Zero balance remains. All venue hire fees and extras have been successfully processed.

Overridden

Manual scheduling completed by senior staff that intentionally bypassed typical room clash checks.

Interaction

A timestamped record logging communications (calls, email notes, face-to-face meetings) in the CRM.

Recurring Series

A block booking sequence linked together repeating at weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly intervals.

BACS

Direct bank-to-bank electronic transfer system common for invoice payouts in the UK.

Outstanding Balance

Any remaining portion of the venue invoice that has not been confirmed via cash, card, or BACS ledger.

Parent Room

A large bookable space that can be sub-divided into independently hireable sections (e.g. Main Hall). Booking the parent blocks all its partitions.

Partition / Child Room

A named section of a parent room — e.g. "North Half", "2nd Third", "3rd Quarter". Has its own rate, capacity, and calendar. Non-overlapping partitions can be booked simultaneously.

Anchor Space

Alternative term for a parent room — the full physical space that partitions are anchored to and divided from.

Room Operating Hours

Optional open/close times set per room or partition in Admin Config. Bookings outside these hours are automatically rejected with a clear message.

Footprint Overlap

When two partitions physically share space — e.g. "North Half" [0–50%] and "1st Quarter" [0–25%] overlap at [0–25%]. The system detects this automatically and blocks conflicting bookings.
